# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge
We pledge to make participation in the `devup` community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of background, identity, or experience level.

## Our Standards
- Use welcoming and inclusive language.
- Be respectful of differing viewpoints and constructive in code reviews.
- Accept responsibility and apologize for mistakes; focus on what is best for the community.
- Harassment, personal attacks, trolling, or publishing private information are unacceptable.

## Enforcement
Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, issues, and PRs that violate this Code of Conduct, or ban contributors temporarily or permanently for severe or repeated violations. Report concerns via GitHub Security/Maintainer contact on <https://github.com/devup-cli/devup>.
